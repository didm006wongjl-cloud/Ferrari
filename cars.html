Iconic Ferrari F1 Cars

From the 375 that won Ferrari's first Grand Prix in 1951 to today's SF-26, these are some of the most famous cars to wear the Prancing Horse.

Ferrari Legends

The Prancing Horse in Motion

Red cars, fast laps and the Tifosi, from the classics to the SF-26.

375 F1 (1951)

Jose Froilan Gonzalez drove the 375 to Ferrari's first World Championship win at the 1951 British Grand Prix at Silverstone.

312T (1975 to 1980)

Designed by Mauro Forghieri with a flat-12 engine. The 312T family won drivers' titles for Niki Lauda in 1975 and 1977 and Jody Scheckter in 1979.

F2002

One of the most dominant F1 cars ever. Michael Schumacher won the 2002 drivers' title with six races still to go.

Record Breakers

F2004

The F2004 won 15 of the 18 races in 2004. Schumacher won 13 of them on his way to his seventh and final world title.

F1-75

Ferrari's 2022 car brought the team back to winning races. Charles Leclerc won in Bahrain, Australia and Austria that year.

SF-26

Ferrari's 2026 car, built for the new hybrid and active aero rules. Hamilton and Leclerc have each won one race with it so far.
