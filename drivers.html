Drivers and the SF-26

Ferrari's 2026 line-up is Charles Leclerc (#16) and seven-time champion Lewis Hamilton (#44). They race the SF-26, Ferrari's first car for the new 2026 rules.

2026 Season

Charles Leclerc #16

From Monaco, born 1997. A Ferrari driver since 2019. He has 9 Grand Prix wins, including Monza in 2019 and 2024, his home race in Monaco in 2024, and the 2026 British Grand Prix.

Lewis Hamilton #44

From the United Kingdom, born 1985. Seven-time World Champion (2008, 2014, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020). Joined Ferrari in 2025 after 12 seasons at Mercedes. Holds the record for most wins with 106.

Team Leaders

Team principal: Fred Vasseur (since 2023). Chassis technical director: Loic Serra. Power unit technical director: Enrico Gualtieri. Official team name: Scuderia Ferrari HP.

SF-26 Tech Specs

V6

Ferrari 067/6 power unit: a 1.6-litre V6 turbo hybrid, limited to 15,000 rpm. Built in Maranello for the new 2026 engine rules.

770 kg

Minimum weight with the driver. Carbon fibre chassis, 8-speed gearbox and push-rod suspension at the front and rear.

2026

First run on 23 January 2026 at Ferrari's Fiorano track. The SF-26 is a new design for the 2026 rules, with active aerodynamics and a bigger electric power share.
