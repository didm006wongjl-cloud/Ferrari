Maranello & Fiorano

The home of Scuderia Ferrari in northern Italy. The team designs and builds its cars and engines here, tests them on its own track, and welcomes fans to the Ferrari museum.

Iconic Cars

The Factory

Ferrari moved to Maranello in 1943. Today the F1 cars, chassis and power units are all designed and built at the Gestione Sportiva racing department here.

Fiorano Circuit

Fiorano is a 3 km test track built in 1972 right next to the factory. Ferrari uses it for shakedowns, filming days and car launches.

Museo Ferrari

The Museo Ferrari in Maranello shows famous F1 cars, trophies and Ferrari road cars. It is one of the most visited museums in the area.

Ferrari Facts

Tifosi

Ferrari fans around the world are called the Tifosi, an Italian word for passionate sports supporters. They follow the team to every race.

Monza

The Italian Grand Prix at Monza is Ferrari's home race. When a Ferrari wins there, the Tifosi flood the track and fill the area under the podium with red.

1947

The Prancing Horse badge comes from Italian WWI pilot Francesco Baracca. Enzo Ferrari added a yellow background, the colour of his home city Modena. The first car to wear the Ferrari name was built in 1947.
