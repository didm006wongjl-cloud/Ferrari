Scuderia Ferrari F1

The oldest and most successful team in Formula 1. Racing since 1950, based in Maranello, Italy. 15 drivers' titles, 16 constructors' titles and 250 Grand Prix wins.

Our History

Founded in 1929

Enzo Ferrari started Scuderia Ferrari in Modena in 1929 to race Alfa Romeo cars. The team moved to Maranello in 1943.

Every Season Since 1950

Ferrari is the only team to race in every Formula 1 World Championship season. Its first race was the 1950 Monaco Grand Prix.

Home of the Prancing Horse

The team builds its cars and engines in Maranello and tests them at its own Fiorano track. Fans around the world are called the Tifosi.

Ferrari by the Numbers

250

Grand Prix wins, more than any other team. The first came at the 1951 British Grand Prix with Jose Froilan Gonzalez. The 250th came at the 2026 British Grand Prix with Charles Leclerc.

16

Constructors' Championships, a record. The most recent was in 2008.

15

Drivers' Championships won by 9 different drivers, from Alberto Ascari in 1952 to Kimi Raikkonen in 2007.
