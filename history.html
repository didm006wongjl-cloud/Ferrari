History and Records

From Enzo Ferrari's first team in 1929 to 250 Grand Prix wins. Ferrari has raced in every Formula 1 season since the World Championship began in 1950.

Ferrari Timeline

Key moments from almost 100 years of Scuderia Ferrari.
1929 to 1951: The Beginning
Modena, then Maranello
Key moments
  • 1929: Enzo Ferrari founds Scuderia Ferrari in Modena to race Alfa Romeo cars.
  • 1950: Ferrari enters the first Formula 1 World Championship.
  • 1951: First Grand Prix win, at Silverstone with Jose Froilan Gonzalez.
1952 to 2008: Championship Years
15 drivers' titles and 16 constructors' titles
Key moments
  • 1952-53: Alberto Ascari wins Ferrari's first two drivers' titles.
  • 1975-79: Niki Lauda wins in 1975 and 1977, Jody Scheckter in 1979.
  • 2000-08: Schumacher wins five titles in a row, Raikkonen wins in 2007.
2009 to Today: The Modern Era
Alonso, Vettel, Leclerc, Sainz and Hamilton
Key moments
  • 2010-14: Fernando Alonso is runner-up three times.
  • 2015-20: Sebastian Vettel wins 14 races for Ferrari.
  • 2019-24: Charles Leclerc wins at Monza and Monaco.
  • 2026: Hamilton and Leclerc win in Barcelona and Silverstone.

All-Time Records

250

Grand Prix wins, more than any other team. The first came at the 1951 British Grand Prix with Jose Froilan Gonzalez. The 250th came at the 2026 British Grand Prix with Charles Leclerc.

16

Constructors' titles, a record: 1961, 1964, 1975, 1976, 1977, 1979, 1982, 1983, 1999, 2000, 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2007 and 2008.

15

Drivers' titles: Ascari (1952, 1953), Fangio (1956), Hawthorn (1958), Phil Hill (1961), Surtees (1964), Lauda (1975, 1977), Scheckter (1979), Schumacher (2000 to 2004) and Raikkonen (2007).
