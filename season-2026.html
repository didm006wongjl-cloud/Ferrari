After 16 of 23 rounds (to the Bahrain Grand Prix, held at Sepang in Malaysia on 4 October 2026), Ferrari is 2nd in the Constructors' Championship with 405 points, 151 behind Mercedes. Ferrari has 2 wins this year.

Meet the Drivers

Hamilton's First Ferrari Win

Lewis Hamilton won the Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix on 14 June 2026. It was his first Grand Prix win for Ferrari and his 106th career win.

250th Win at Silverstone

Charles Leclerc won the British Grand Prix on 5 July 2026. It was Ferrari's 250th Grand Prix win, the first team to reach this number.
